## Narratives in Motion

**The compute scramble is becoming the story.** Google's $40B Anthropic deal, Meta's $115–135B capex, OpenAI's multi-hundred-billion compute commitments across Cerebras and beyond — frontier labs are now functionally infrastructure companies whose moat is power, fab access, and multi-year GPU contracts more than weights. The "model wars" framing is starting to feel naive; it's a balance-sheet war.

**Production teams are ripping out agent frameworks.** The pattern showing up across blog posts and dev community writing this month: teams evaluate a framework, build a prototype, ship to production, hit a wall the abstractions make harder to debug, then rewrite the core loop in plain code. Microsoft's new Agent Framework 1.0 and the A2A protocol are landing into a market where the biggest sentiment is suspicion of frameworks. ([Medium](https://medium.com/@jmelethil/most-ai-agent-frameworks-are-solving-the-wrong-problem-ee64e1f9830e))

**The reasoning-vs-reliability tension is now empirical.** The ICLR 2026 paper showing that harder reasoning training increases tool hallucination is concrete evidence for what builders have been saying anecdotally — the more you push models toward chain-of-thought, the more confidently they invent calls. With 96% of enterprises running agents, this is suddenly a procurement-level question, not a research curiosity. ([Asanify](https://asanify.com/blog/news/ai-agent-hallucination-april-29-2026/))

## Hot Takes Floating Around

- "Most AI agent frameworks are solving the wrong problem" — they optimize for orchestration syntax when the real bottleneck is debugging and observability. ([Medium](https://medium.com/@jmelethil/most-ai-agent-frameworks-are-solving-the-wrong-problem-ee64e1f9830e))
- The real winner of April 2026 isn't a model — it's the open-source ecosystem. A free self-hostable model beating the best closed offering on coding benchmarks ends "closed-model dominance" for anyone willing to run their own infra.
- "Command Marketing" is replacing assistant-style AI: human leaders set objectives, agents handle the full execution loop. The org chart is starting to flatten in places where this lands.
- The reasoning-training tradeoff is now public: smarter models hallucinate tools more confidently. Anyone running agents in production needs to account for this in their evals.
- April 2026 is when AI stopped being experimental infrastructure and started being just infrastructure — and the winners are the teams shipping the boring stuff (edge cases, retries, monitoring), not the teams with the biggest models.
